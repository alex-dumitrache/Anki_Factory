  * Everything above is what `raw_chat.txt` looks like: one whole chat session,
    pasted. The opening block is the real setup prompt. The lecture content is

  * Note what the setup prompt is doing: it tells the model to treat "create a

    meant to be captured, not obeyed - the pipeline acts on them later.

  * Each request must sit on ONE line. The trigger match is line-by-line, so a
    request that wraps onto a second line is cut off at the line break.

  * Stage 1 (`run_phase1.py`) then does three things:
      1. deletes the setup block, from "You are operating in transcription-only
         mode" through "continue responding with 'Received.'";
      2. strips every standalone "You said:", "ChatGPT said:" and "Received.";
      3. lifts out every line starting "create a flashcard" / "create flashcards"
         as a card trigger, and treats everything else as the lecturer's words.

  * If you capture lectures some other way, change those regexes at the top of
    `run_phase1.py`. Nothing downstream depends on the chat format.
